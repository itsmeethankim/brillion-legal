Brillion Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 · Updated November 21, 2026 (friends, usernames, notifications)
The short version. Brillion is a trivia game. We keep what's needed to run it: an account ID, the answers you play and your scores, and, if you use Friends, a username you choose. We don't ask for your real name or email, we don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising. Answers you type are checked by an AI service. You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app.

Who we are

Brillion ("we", "us") makes the Brillion app for iPhone and the Brillion website. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the game, and the choices you have. Questions: itsmeethankim@icloud.com.

What other players see

  • Everyone sees how common answers are (for example "12% of divers said Penguin"), counted across all players without names.
  • In a duel, you and the friend you duel see each other's accepted answers and scores, after both of you have played.
  • Your custom topic packs are private. Only you, and a friend you duel, can play them.
  • Your friends see your username, your daily score and tiers (only after they've played that day themselves), whether you found a Brillionaire, your weekly total and your streak. People who aren't your friends see none of this.
  • Your username is shown to players you send a friend request or duel to. You can block a player at any time: blocked players can't see you, request you or duel you. You can also report a username; we review reports and remove names that break our rules.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Brillion. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Supabase hosts our database, accounts and server functions (United States).
  • OpenAI checks whether answers fit a prompt, writes prompts and answer lists, and screens custom topics for harmful content. We send the prompt, its rules and the answer or topic you typed, never your account ID. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data isn't used to train their models.
  • Expo (650 Industries) relays push notifications to Apple. It receives your device's push token and the notification text.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions. It receives your Brillion account ID and your App Store purchase records.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and App Store payments, under Apple's privacy policy.

We don't sell your personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the safety of our players and service.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and gameplay are kept until you delete your account.
  • Custom topic packs that nobody has played for 90 days are deleted automatically.
  • Server logs are kept only as long as our providers need them for security and operations.
  • Aggregate answer counts (how many players gave an answer) don't identify anyone and may be kept after an account is deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, plays, answers, topic packs, username, friends, requests, blocks, notification tokens and Pro record. If you subscribe, also cancel in your App Store settings, since Apple manages billing.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask any privacy question, by emailing itsmeethankim@icloud.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as data portability or objecting to processing; we honor these requests.
  • You can play without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional.

Children

Brillion isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Brillion, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to our database is restricted to the game's servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
