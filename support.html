Brillion Support

Help for the Brillion daily trivia app

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email brillionapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

  • How do I play? Each day has six prompts. You have 20 seconds to type an answer that fits. Rarer answers sink your brain deeper; an answer nobody else gave is a Brillionaire.
  • Restore Brillion Pro. In the app, open Me, then Settings, then Restore purchases, while signed in to the Apple ID that bought it.
  • Cancel Brillion Pro. iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, then Brillion. Or in the app: Me, Settings, Manage subscription.
  • Keep my progress on a new phone. In Me, tap Continue with Apple. Sign in with the same Apple ID on the new phone.
  • Report or block a player. Tap Report or Block next to any username.
  • Delete my account. Me, then Settings, then Delete account. This removes your dives, friends and username for good.
